# Model Card — Churn Model 1.0.0

## Intended use

Prioritize telecom customers for human-reviewed retention outreach and help analysts understand risk drivers. The model is a decision-support component, not an automated adverse-decision system.

## Data status

All 12,000 records are synthetic and generated by `backend/app/synthetic.py`. They do not describe LTT customers, network performance, revenue, or actual churn.

## Candidate models

- Logistic Regression
- Random Forest
- XGBoost

The selection score weights ROC-AUC (40%), Recall (30%), Precision (20%), and F1 (10%). Accuracy is reported for context but excluded from selection.

## Current synthetic validation result

The current reproducible seed selected Logistic Regression:

- ROC-AUC: 0.746
- Recall: 0.506
- Precision: 0.305
- F1: see `backend/artifacts/model_metadata.json` after training
- Synthetic churn prevalence: 11.6%

Class weighting is used during training. Probabilities are adjusted back to the observed training prevalence to avoid presenting class-balanced scores as population probabilities.

## Explainability

- Global drivers: mean absolute SHAP impact, aggregated to original features.
- Individual explanations: top three deterministic reason codes based on operational signals.
- Recommended actions: transparent business rules tied to service, support, usage, payment, satisfaction, and value signals.

## Limitations and production gates

- No claim of performance on real LTT data.
- 60/90-day values are cumulative scenario horizons derived from the 30-day probability, not separately trained survival models.
- Geographic coordinates are approximate visualization anchors, not customer locations.
- Historical trends and historical retention outcomes are clearly marked simulated.
- Before production: agree the churn label/window, validate leakage and calibration, audit segment fairness, choose threshold from intervention capacity/cost, integrate the enterprise IdP, monitor drift, and establish human override and rollback procedures.
